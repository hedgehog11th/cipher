# Cipher

A-Level past-paper question bank. First release: CAIE Biology 9700, A2 Paper 4, organised by the
2025–2027 syllabus. Later: Physics, Chemistry, Further Mathematics.

## Who and where

Students revising on a computer or an iPad, usually one question at a time, at a desk. Phones are for
emergencies. The site is English only. No login: custom tags (planned) live in the browser.

## What a visitor does

1. Finds a question: by chapter on the contents page, or filtered by topic or year (search planned).
2. Reads the whole question (all its parts) and tries it on paper.
3. Opens the marking for one part at a time: the published mark scheme, an AI supplement that explains
   the method, the syllabus topic, and the examiner report's own words.

## Rules that shape the design

- One whole question is one record. Parts are never listed or counted on their own.
- Mark scheme and examiner report text are shown as published. AI-written text is always labelled
  "AI supplement" and never presented as the examiners' view.
- Answers stay closed until the visitor asks for them.
- Content is written and reviewed by the owner; questions appear only when `review.status` is approved.

## Out of scope for V1

Accounts, payment, AI tutor, question generator, weakness detection, revision paths, live AI calls,
social features, recommendations.
